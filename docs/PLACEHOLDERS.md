
## Event photos (added 25 November 2026)

49 photos supplied by MSB Events are in `public/photos` (originals in `reference/photos`). They go into the gallery database like the posters: an admin presses **Load MSB events** in Dashboard → Events, and can then remove any of them in Dashboard → Gallery. Page title bands (Events, Gallery, About, Become a vendor, Cart, My tickets, Profile, legal pages) use some of these photos as backgrounds.
Labels come only from what is visible in the photos:
- **Fun Walk 2025**: the shirts say "MSB Fun Walk & Family Day 2025".
- **Soulful Blow 2026** and **Spring Blow 2026 activations**: the photos carry those logos.
- **Summer Blow**: the stage banners read Summer Blow / MSB. Year not known.
- **Spring Blow, Soulful Blow and Spin Blow (car spinning)**: the event is inferred from the scene. Year not known.
- **"MSB events"**: performer, DJ and crowd shots whose event and year are not known.

Please send the correct event and year for any undated photo, along with artist names if you want captions to name performers.
